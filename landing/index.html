A small floating assistant that solves questions from your screen.

YO sits in the corner of your desktop as a tiny 210px pill. When you are working on a coding problem, math question, or reading documentation, click one button or press a shortcut. It captures your screen, asks Gemini Vision, and shows the solution directly over your workspace without making you switch windows.

When and why this is useful

Everyday scenarios where opening a separate browser tab or copying code back and forth is inconvenient.

💻

Coding, LeetCode & Debugging

Stuck on a tricky edge case, compiler error, or algorithm problem? Press Ctrl+Shift+S. YO reads the error message or code from your screen and gives you the exact fix immediately.

🛡️

Screen Sharing Without Being Seen

When you are in a Google Meet, Zoom, or Discord meeting and sharing your screen, YO uses native Windows capture exclusion so other participants cannot see YO at all. No awkward black box appears.

🎯

Typing Without Losing Focus

Clicking on the capsule or taking a screenshot does not deactivate Chrome, Antigravity, or VS Code. Your text cursor stays in your active code editor without window flickering or taskbar blinking.

How it works in 3 steps

Step 01

Launch Portable .exe

Download and run the executable. Enter your Google Gemini API key once. It stores locally on your machine and opens in the compact capsule dock.

Step 02

Snap or Solve

Press Ctrl+Shift+S to solve immediately, or click 📸 to queue multiple screenshots (up to 5) before sending.

Step 03

Copy Answer & Continue

The solution appears in the small pop-up bubble above the capsule. Click the copy icon to grab the code and paste it right into your project.

Global Keyboard Shortcuts

These hotkeys work globally from anywhere on Windows:

Shortcut What it does
Ctrl + Shift + S Instant Snap & Solve (captures primary screen and streams the answer into the capsule bubble)
Ctrl + Alt + S Snap Screenshot into Queue (adds to the 1-5 screenshot queue without solving yet)
Ctrl + Shift + Space Show / Hide the overlay completely
Ctrl + Shift + G Switch between the mini 210px Stealth Capsule and the full Chat window
Ctrl + V Paste any image or text from clipboard directly into the assistant

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about AI exam solving, screen sharing, and privacy.

Is YO Assistant truly undetectable on Google Meet and Zoom screen sharing?

Yes. Traditional tools use basic protection which draws a noticeable black box in video streams. YO Assistant applies native Windows Win32 SetWindowDisplayAffinity(WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE = 0x11) across all native HWND surfaces. The screen share stream sees directly through YO to your background desktop.

Does clicking or using YO trigger a "Tab Switch" or "Window Blur" event in online test browsers?

No. YO Assistant runs a background foreground tracker. Whenever you click the capsule, press hotkeys, or drag the dock, YO automatically reasserts active focus to your background browser (Chrome / Edge), preventing test portals from detecting focus loss.

Is there any catch, subscription, or registration required?

Zero catch. YO Assistant is free and open-source under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, no tracking telemetry, and no account registrations. You provide your own personal Google Gemini API key.

Which models and question types does it support?

It connects to Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and Gemini 3.7 Flash multimodal vision models, capable of solving coding questions (Python, C++, Java, JS), calculus, mathematical proofs, diagrams, multiple choice questions, and system architecture problems.

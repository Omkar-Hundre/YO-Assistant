🛡️ Key Concepts & Terminologies

Stealth Capsule

Compact, shadowless floating pill dock that stays persistently on top without being detected or blocking screen content.

Zero-Focus-Loss Architecture

Clovi uses native WS_EX_NOACTIVATE and focusable: false. Background browsers (Chrome/Edge) remain 100% active; window.onblur and visibilitychange never fire.

Invisible Screen Share

Using Windows DWM WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE, Clovi is rendered invisible to Zoom, Teams, Meet, Discord, and browser tab captures.

Auto-Clipboard Injection

Copy any text (Ctrl+C) and press F9 or click [📋 From Clipboard] to automatically append instructions without typing.

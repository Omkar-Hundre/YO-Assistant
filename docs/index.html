How Clovi works
See it. Snap it. Solved.
Three keystrokes between you and the answer. No switching tabs, no typing, no wasted time.
1

Launch Clovi (always on top)

Clovi starts as an ultra-compact 210px capsule pinned at the top of your screen. It is excluded from all screen captures at the OS kernel level.

2

Press Ctrl+Shift+S to snap

One global hotkey captures whatever question, code, or diagram is on screen — without ever losing focus on your exam browser.

3

Get the direct answer streamed instantly

Gemini Vision reads the problem and streams the direct answer, runnable code, or step-by-step math straight into the floating bubble above your screen.

Undetectable in every way
Built to be invisible.
Using native Win32 Display Affinity APIs that completely exclude the window from screen capturing software.
🫥

Invisible to screen share

Uses Windows WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE — Clovi never appears in Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, or OBS recordings.

🔒

Zero focus loss

Clovi uses WS_EX_NOACTIVATE so clicking on it never blurs your exam browser — no tab switch events, no focus-loss warnings.

📡

Invisible to proctors

No DOM injection, no browser extension, no background process attached to your exam tab. It lives completely outside the browser.

🎯

Follows your eyes

Drag the capsule anywhere on screen. Position it at the exact corner that's most comfortable for quick glances without moving your head.

🌑

No telemetry

No sign-in, no analytics, no data sent to third parties. 100% local Electron app. Your API key and snapshots stay private on your machine.

💾

Portable — no installer

Single .exe file. Drop it anywhere, run it, done. Nothing written to Windows Registry. Works on any Windows 10/11 machine.
